- **1980→2000:** births per woman fell 19%. Marriage part -0.278, within-marriage part +0.064 (log points).
- **1980→2020:** births per woman fell 27%. Marriage part -0.406, within-marriage part +0.084.
- Age-by-age check, 1980→2005 (Kitagawa decomposition over five 5-year age groups): TFR 1.73 → 1.25 (-0.48); fewer married women -0.80, births per married woman +0.31, so **165% of the decline came from marriage**.
- Across prefectures, 1985→2020: the marriage part is larger than the total decline in 40 of 47 prefectures, because births *per married woman* rose there. Median share of the decline due to marriage: 146%.
- Caution: births per married woman can rise partly because the women who still marry are the ones who want children (selection), so the within-marriage part is a lower bound on how much family size itself fell.
